# 2. Your issue

Pick a group from the table in
[`../groups/README.md`](../groups/README.md). Each group is one
GitHub issue and one brief, `groups/0N-*.md`: the problem, where to look,
the first job, and the definition of done.

## 2.1 The issue thread

Look in the thread for:

- a maintainer saying how they want it fixed. I asked the design question
  in advance, the answer may be there.
- a linked PR or an assignee: the issue is taken; tell me and I'll find
  you another one.

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Nobody else is assigned and no PR is linked
